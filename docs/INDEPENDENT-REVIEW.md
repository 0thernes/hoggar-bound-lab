# Independently checked research companion

This companion was developed and checked alongside the main Hoggar Bound Lab
experiment on **2026-11-09**. It contributes an exact Lean certificate, independent
matrix and stack checks, a critical literature review, and an operational browser
explorer. It is an AI-assisted research artifact, not a peer-reviewed publication.

**Start with the [interactive explorer](independent-review/index.html),
[research note](paper/independent-research-note.md), and
[formal audit](results/lean-audit.md).**

## What the evidence supports

- The general induced-Pauli-context cubic spectral bound is checked in Lean 4.
- An explicit three-qubit assignment violates **exactly 63 of 315** contexts, and
  every assignment violates at least 63. Both sides are checked in the same Lean
  model, including an exact finite certificate evaluated by the kernel.
- Additional checked lower bounds are **1451** (four-qubit full space), **28786**
  (five-qubit full space), **315** (four-qubit real quadric), and **6426**
  (five-qubit real quadric). The last excludes 4743. Upper-bound witnesses for the
  larger instances are separately recounted; their optimality is not inferred.
- The independent matrix checker agrees on **138,792 contexts across 12
  geometries**, recounts eight witnesses, and checks 120 sparse supports.
- Moonlab, the two specified QGTL differential-geometry kernels, the seeded
  software RNG, and Eshkol arithmetic have separately replayed checks. Their
  precise scope and hashes are in [the stack audit](results/stack-independent-audit.md).
- The browser explorer's five profiles pass 750 incremental-update comparisons,
  monotone local-search checks, and witness-export checks. Its counters use all
  contexts even when its graph draws a capped subset.

## Important corrections to the working notebook

`NOTES.md` is a historical working record and is not the final priority or proof
statement. The [literature audit](results/literature-audit.md) supersedes its
unchecked priority/status language:

1. The scalar inequality is the classical **Okumura inequality**. The identical
   spectral-floor formula, its paired-spectrum refinement, and its three-family
   applications already appear in B. W. Jonah's 21 October 2026 preprint,
   [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23538829](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23538829),
   Lemma 5 and Theorem 6. These are not discoveries of this lab. Our arbitrary
   induced-set statement relaxes that preprint's stated line-closure hypothesis;
   priority for that relaxation or the formalization is not asserted.
2. An October 2026 preprint already claims exact degrees **315, 1575, and 6975**
   for the four-qubit elliptic, four-qubit full, and five-qubit real configurations.
   Its supplied certificate verifier was replayed successfully; its full exhaustive
   census was not independently rerun. Do not advertise our weaker lower bounds
   as the first solution or strongest current result for those configurations.
3. Kramers reduction requires the signed operator to be fixed by the antiunitary;
   setwise invariance alone is insufficient. Its equality object is a rank-two
   normalized projector, rather than the pure-state equality object of the
   unreduced bound. This refinement is outside the audited Lean development.
4. The full seven-qubit integer lower bound is **9,227,609**.
5. The general full-Pauli asymptotic statement concerns a different family from
   the repeated-graph asymptotic question in Muller and Saniga's 2026 paper.

World-first priority and worldwide recognition have **not** been established.
The defensible contribution is the general formalization and independently
checked reproduction, with explicit certificates and a working exploratory
artifact. The spectral formula itself has verified prior art.

## Reproduction and sources

The [explorer README](independent-review/README.md) gives executable commands.
The [Lean audit](results/lean-audit.md) identifies all 22 audited declarations,
the standard axiom allowlist, exact source hashes, and necessary build commands.
The isolated formal additions must be built explicitly; building only the original
root library does not check them. The CI workflow repeats these checks from source.

The [research note](paper/independent-research-note.md) gives the derivation and
primary-source references. The companion PDF is generated by
`scripts/independent_paper_build.py` with proper LaTeX equations; the analytic
[figure](paper/independent-figures/spectral-bounds.pdf) is regenerated by
`scripts/independent_figures.py`.
