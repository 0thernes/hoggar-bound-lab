What does this settle?

A carrier is the signed sum of the observables in a hyperplane section. Its spectrum records its eigenvalues. The completion matrix records signed contexts connecting points outside that section. Equal carrier spectra do not always determine equal completion spectra.

This explicit pair establishes the four-qubit C3 instance of Conjecture E in B. W. Jonah’s The reach of the section-square law, v0.5, which listed that instance as open. It supports that conjecture, which predicts failure of the strict spectral law. The result does not settle the conjecture in every dimension.

The browser checks exact arithmetic. The linked research artifact provides independent Python constructions, a Lean formalization with its stated scope, and reproducibility instructions. This is a research preprint; historical priority and peer review are separate from mathematical verification.
