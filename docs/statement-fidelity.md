# Statement Fidelity Audit Table (`hoggar-bound-lab`)

**Purpose:** Lean 4's kernel guarantees that a proof establishes *its formal Lean statement* under `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. It cannot verify that the formal statement faithfully matches the informal theorem in the paper or the conventions in the literature. This table maps every headline Lean 4.35.0 declaration in `hoggar-bound-lab` line by line to its informal claim, literature anchor, non-vacuity witness, and exact epistemic boundary.

All Lean declarations below exist verbatim in this repository and are audited in `lean/VerifyNewFormalizations.lean`, `lean/HoggarBound/Challenge.lean`, or the retained audit files (`C3Verification.lean`, `IndependentVerification.lean`, `SaturatedVerification.lean`, `SaturatedSevenVerification.lean`, `CarrierFamily.lean`).
