# Code of Conduct

Be direct, honest, and rigorous about mathematics, code, and evidence; be respectful toward people.

- **Attack claims and definitions, never people.** Hostile technical scrutiny of proofs, definitions, citations, and scripts is explicitly welcomed.
- **No fabrication or undisclosed AI spam.** Disclose AI assistance honestly; never claim a check passed without running it.
- **No harassment, threats, doxxing, or personal abuse** in issues, discussions, or email.

Report conduct concerns directly to the maintainer at `0_0@0thernes.art`.
