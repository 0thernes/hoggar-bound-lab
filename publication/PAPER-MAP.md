# Source papers for the planned combined manuscript

The author has requested **one combined paper**, planned as version **1.2.0**, integrating the spectral-floor/saturation study and the four-qubit C3 study. The author is **Alexander S. Donahue, 0thernes LLC**. The two source research papers and the independent verification companion remain preserved as historical artifacts. The separate chats used different AI research workflows; their contributions will be combined with their distinct theorem and evidence boundaries intact.

The intended narrative connects the spectral floor and configurations attaining it with the information that a carrier spectrum fails to retain about a completion. This page records the source identities and comparison baseline; it does not claim that the planned combined manuscript has already been built, verified, released, or submitted.

## Which journal package belongs to which paper?

The other chat's [Journal of Physics A manuscript and supplement](https://github.com/0thernes/hoggar-bound-lab/tree/c656f52aef29b21f97e0daa241915aa7fbc0de2b/publication/spectral-floor/jphysa) belong to the **spectral-floor study**. Their [build record](https://github.com/0thernes/hoggar-bound-lab/blob/c656f52aef29b21f97e0daa241915aa7fbc0de2b/publication/spectral-floor/jphysa/build-record.json) names `paper/spectral-floor.md` as their source. The manuscript retains the research, while the supplement contains the Tsotchke demonstration, tests, reproduction instructions, and witness format. These are two components of one journal package, not additional research papers.

[JPHYS-A-SUBMISSION-DRAFT.md](JPHYS-A-SUBMISSION-DRAFT.md) is the **earlier preparation draft for the C3 paper**. Its title and abstract identify the carrier/completion result. Both that draft and `publication/spectral-floor/JPHYSA-SUBMISSION.md` predate the decision to combine the studies. The new plan is **one coherent manuscript and one corresponding journal package**, with supplementary material as needed. It is not a plan for two separate journal submissions. Prepared files and GitHub releases do not constitute submission; [publication status](STATUS.md) records actual milestones. The same manuscript must not be submitted twice through separate chats.

The labels `v1.1.1` and `v0.2.0-c3-certificate` identify different historical release records. A larger version number does not mean that one source paper was merely a revision of the other. The planned 1.2.0 manuscript combines their contributions; it does not retroactively change their frozen proofs or publication records. Neither source study is represented here as peer reviewed.
