## Author responsibilities

arXiv requires disclosure of significant generative-AI use and makes each author responsible for the
contents, including errors and incorrect references. AI tools cannot be authors. The paper discloses
its AI use; computational checks do not replace the author's critical review. Moderation is separate
from peer review. [Official arXiv policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html#AI-language-tools).

Zenodo requires a human-conducted research basis and disclosure of substantive AI assistance. Formal
correctness alone does not settle that eligibility question. Where applicability remains uncertain,
its policy directs depositors to support. [Official Zenodo policy](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/13/227).

Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical permits disclosed AI research assistance while
requiring actual author contribution, critical revision, final approval and accountability. Use the
prepared cover letter only after its author-controlled statements are completed accurately. See the
[venue guidance](../ROUTES.md) and [journal draft](JOURNAL-COVER-LETTER.md).
