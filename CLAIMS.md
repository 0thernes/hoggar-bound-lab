# Claims Ledger (`hoggar-bound-lab`)

Every scientific and engineering claim in **Spectral bounds and their limits in Pauli contextuality: exact configurations and a proof of Jonah's Conjecture E** (v1.9.0) is indexed below with its exact epistemic tier, proof or verification command, evidence artifact, and **what would prove us wrong** (falsification criterion, Build Standard B04 & B42).

See also the detailed per-declaration historical provenance map in [docs/CLAIMS.md](docs/CLAIMS.md) and the line-by-line [Statement Fidelity Table](docs/statement-fidelity.md).

## Epistemic Tiers

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `kernel-checked` | Proved inside Lean 4.35.0 (`0 sorry`, `0 admit`, `0 native_decide`, strictly `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`), replayed through `lake comparator --paranoid` (6 kernels) and audited by `VerifyNewFormalizations.lean` or retained audits. |
| `certificate-checked` | Verified in exact integer / rational / symbolic arithmetic (zero floating point) by standalone C11 / Python programs with negative controls, outside the Lean 4 kernel. |
| `paper proof` | Ordinary mathematical derivation in `paper/unified-contextuality.md` without a Lean 4 formalization. |
| `numerical_approximation` | Floating-point computation (explicitly labeled with tolerance; never used as sole proof of an exact claim). |
| `open` | Unresolved mathematical question or external human/legal step; explicitly not claimed. |
